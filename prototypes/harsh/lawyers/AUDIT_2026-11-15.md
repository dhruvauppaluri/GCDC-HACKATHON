# Candidate review audit — November 15, 2026

## Scope and evidence

- Exercised 39 candidate/job pairs from 11 completed fictional profiles and nine curated roles through the quick matching stage.
- Ran the local Qwen3 4B Paralegal on 12 profiles containing resume text. Initial run: 11 completed, one timed out. Reran three profiles after changes, including the timeout; all three completed.
- Ran six complete local Paralegal → two Lawyers → Judge reviews: a matching and mismatched role for hardware, software, and product. Reran the three cases exposing the clearest errors after corrections.
- Ran all 52 backend tests and the existing frontend build. The six full reviews had no independently verified Depth project artifacts, so they test claim handling, citation safety, and abstention, not artifact scoring.

Raw outputs are stored locally in the audit workspace and were not committed because they are large generated test artifacts.

## Guidance for the agents

1. Paralegal: extract short exact spans. Omit career goals. Treat self-ratings as candidate claims. Do not infer an employer from an “Experience” heading. Identify a project only when an artifact or project is described. Missing details stay unknown.
2. Lawyers: a strength or explicit gap needs a citation that supports the whole sentence. If no citation exists, abstain. Do not turn an activity into a verified project or job. Give one specific missing-evidence reason per required qualification; avoid repeated generic objections.
3. Judge: keep required and preferred qualifications separate. An explicit candidate claim is half-credit evidence coverage, not demonstrated proficiency. Unknown is not a failure. If nothing is supported, show `unassessed` rather than a 0–100 range. Keep eligibility separate from fit and do not present the score as hiring odds.

## Remaining limits

- The sample profiles are short synthetic text, not real PDFs. None provides completed, independently verified Depth findings. The full live Depth → Paralegal → Lawyers → Judge path still needs a project artifact and browser rehearsal.
- One of 12 initial Paralegal calls timed out; retry succeeded. Slow local model execution remains a demo risk.
- The citation check verifies IDs and exact text spans but cannot prove every semantic inference. Human review is still required before using a verdict in a real hiring context.
- No Figma link or design export is present in the repo yet. The frontend merge awaits Venya's design handoff and integration review.
